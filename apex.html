THE STICKY-AIM ENGINE

Why our aim locks on and stays there

Most anti-recoil moves the stick on a fixed vertical axis. The game's aim assist works on a rotational (polar) axis. When those two fight, your reticle slides off.

Sniperr models recoil in the same polar space the game does, then layers correction with the aim-assist pull instead of against it. The result is tracking that holds a strafing target through slides, jumps, and beam-strafes.
